summary: This conference report from the World Resources Institute (WRI) highlights how to integrate electric mobility and air quality initiatives in Latin America to improve public health and accelerate transport decarbonization. It documents a meeting held in Bogotá from January 27 to 31, 2025, with representatives from WRI Brazil, WRI Colombia, WRI Mexico, and the Global Office, funded by the Ross Center for Sustainable Cities. Through design thinking workshops and field visits, including the Green Móvil depot and maintenance facility housing 406 electric buses, participants identified opportunities for technical collaboration, capacity building, and financing. The report captures technical experiences in air pollutant monitoring and impact assessment, including studies on the electrification of 379 TransMilenio buses, as well as technical assistance provided to Metrobús and freight fleets. As a result, the teams defined a 2030 vision that positions WRI as a leading regional interdisciplinary organization, prioritizes vulnerable populations, and proposes strategic lines of work, including projects in school environments, methodological tools, and roadmaps for scaling up electric mobility and attracting investment.
